### The four real gaps, stated as ungoverned behaviour

1. **The approved verdict never reaches the drafters.** Each consumer is told to carry the *theses*;
   none is told the brief's `## Provisional verdict`. A chapter set whose individual directives are
   each faithful to a thesis can still add up to a posture the approved verdict contradicts — a broadly
   positive sector read where the verdict said `Selective`. Every directive passes its own check, and
   nothing downstream compares the aggregate to the verdict.
2. **The change conditions never reach the drafters.** `## What would change the view` marks which
   parts of the view are contingent. A drafter that never sees it writes a contingent thesis as settled,
   and the hedging the brief called for silently disappears.
3. **No authority-conflict rule anywhere.** A drafter that finds the evidence no longer supports an
   approved thesis has no instruction. It can write around the thesis or follow it against the
   evidence, and either way nothing records that a conflict existed. This is the one gap the project's
   own prose already worries about — `run-synthesis.md:71` says *"no downstream QC step recovers the
   difference"* — without closing it.
4. **Compliance QC validates against the directive, not the approved brief.** Because directives are
   themselves derived from judgment, a chapter that drifted from the approved view while matching its
   directive passes QC. The QC is never given the brief and never asked to check drift or
   evidence-permission overreach against it.
