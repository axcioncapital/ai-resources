# Boat park review, 2018

Twelve pages of correspondence about trailer spaces, condensed by someone to the note
below and then never looked at again.

The review concluded that the boat park was full, that it had been full since 2014, and
that the club should either buy the adjoining field or stop taking new trailer members.
Neither happened. The waiting list is still kept on paper by whoever is on the gate.
