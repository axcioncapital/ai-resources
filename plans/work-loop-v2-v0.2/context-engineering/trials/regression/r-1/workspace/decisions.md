# Club decisions

Settled by the committee and recorded here. An entry stands unless a later entry
supersedes it in as many words.

## 2026-06-04 — Payment handling

The club does not hold card details. Anything involving money goes through the payment
provider we already use for boat-park fees. This is not negotiable and is not to be
revisited as part of any renewal work.

## 2026-04-22 — Membership year

The membership year runs 1 October to 30 September. A renewal is for the whole year
regardless of when in the year it is paid.

## 2026-03-11 — Records stay in the club's own files

Membership records are kept in the club folders, not in a third-party service. The
committee wants to be able to read them without a login in ten years' time.
