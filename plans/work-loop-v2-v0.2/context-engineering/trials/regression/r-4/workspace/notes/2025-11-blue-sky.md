# Blue-sky session, November 2025

An evening of ideas, none of them costed, none of them agreed, written up by whoever had
the pen.

- A touchscreen in the front room where visitors browse the whole collection.
- Partnering with the university so students can transcribe the card index.
- A phone app with a walking trail round the town.
- Selling high-resolution images of the collection to picture libraries.
- Opening the store on the first Saturday of the month.
- A schools loan box scheme.

Nobody took an action from any of this.
