# Trustees' meeting, 11 June 2026

Present: Danaher, Vellacott, Ambrose, Quill, Featherstone. Apologies: Roskilly.

**1. Minutes of the previous meeting.** Agreed as a correct record, proposed Ambrose,
seconded Quill.

**2. Matters arising.** The gutter over the store has been cleared. The quotation for the
second dehumidifier is still awaited from the second supplier; Featherstone to chase.

**3. Treasurer's report.** Balance at 31 May was £14,206. The heating bill came in under
budget by £340 owing to the mild spring. The trustees noted the report.

**4. Front-of-house rota.** Two volunteers have stood down. Ambrose will ask at the
history society. Discussion of whether the museum should open on Mondays in August was
inconclusive and was deferred to September.

**5. County grant.** The grant offer letter has been received and signed. The conditions
attached are mostly the usual ones about acknowledgement in publicity and access for the
county's own officers. One condition is new this year and the trustees should be aware of
it: the county requires that for every object loaned out, the lender's name and full
postal address are retained for six years after the loan ends, and are producible on
request. Featherstone asked whether this affected the catalogue work and the chair said he
would raise it with whoever was doing it.

**6. Shop.** Postcard stock is low. The trustees agreed to reorder the six best-selling
designs and to drop the other four.

**7. Any other business.** Quill raised the state of the labels in the second room. Agreed
to look at it after the catalogue work is further on.

**8. Date of next meeting.** 9 July, 7pm.
