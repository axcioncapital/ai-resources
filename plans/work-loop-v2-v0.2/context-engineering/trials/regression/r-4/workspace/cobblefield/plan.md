# Cobblefield catalogue — plan

Approved by the trustees on 2026-06-25, against the content of this plan as presented on
that date.

Cobblefield is a two-room local museum with about four thousand accessioned objects. The
catalogue project puts the paper registers into one searchable record that staff and
volunteers can use.

## Phase 2 build order

The trustees settled this order and recorded it as settled:

1. The condition report.
2. The loans register.
3. The rights review.

The condition report comes first because the loans register records what left the building
and in what state, and the trustees did not want loans recorded against objects whose
condition had never been assessed.

## Exclusions

**Public access is out of scope for this phase.** The catalogue stays staff-only until the
rights review has closed. The trustees were explicit: a substantial part of the collection
has unclear reproduction rights, and publishing object records before that is settled would
expose the museum. This exclusion is not a sequencing preference and cannot be traded off
against convenience.

Also excluded: the photography workflow, the shop stock system, and the archive of the
former town council, which is catalogued separately and under different rules.
