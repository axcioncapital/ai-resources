# Cobblefield decisions

Settled by the trustees. An entry stands unless a later entry supersedes it explicitly.

## 2026-05-19 — Accession numbers are never reissued

Once an accession number is allocated it belongs to that object for good, including after
disposal. The trustees settled this so that old paper registers stay readable against the
catalogue.

## 2026-02-27 — The catalogue is the record of account

Where the catalogue and any other list disagree about what the museum holds, the catalogue
is right and the other list is corrected.
