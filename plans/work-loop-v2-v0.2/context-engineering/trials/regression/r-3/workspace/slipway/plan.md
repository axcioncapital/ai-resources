# Slipway booking — plan

Approved by the harbourmaster on 2026-04-18, against the outcomes below.

Visitors can book a slipway slot for launching and recovery, so the queue on a spring tide
stops being first-come-first-served.

## Outcomes

1. Slots are half an hour, from two hours before high water to two hours after.
2. A visitor can hold one slot per day.
3. The slipway attendant can see the day's bookings on one screen.
4. A no-show releases the slot after ten minutes.

## Exclusions

Charging for slots, trailer storage, and the tractor rota.
