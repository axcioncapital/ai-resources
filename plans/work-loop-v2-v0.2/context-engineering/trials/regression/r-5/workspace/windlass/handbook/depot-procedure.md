# Depot procedure

Unlock the roller door at 06:30. Cages come off the trunker between 06:45 and 07:15. Sort
to route on the floor markings. Vans pull onto the bay in route order. The last van away
signs the board.

Fire exits are kept clear at all times. The first-aid box is in the office by the kettle.
