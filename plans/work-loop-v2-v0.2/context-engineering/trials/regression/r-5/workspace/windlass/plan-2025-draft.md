# Windlass — draft plan

Drafted November 2025. Never approved. Kept for reference; governs nothing.

Windlass is the dispatch board for the van round. It replaces the whiteboard in the depot
office.

## Proposed build order

1. The scan-in screen.
2. The driver's handheld view.
3. The van loading sheet.
4. End-of-day reconciliation.

## Proposed exclusions

Customer tracking and invoicing.

## Working assumptions

A van takes twenty-two drops on a normal day. Cut-off at the depot is 16:30 in winter and
17:00 in summer.
