# Develop mode

Use Develop to turn a destination-authorised package into a new substantive asset or section. This mode owns material development and composition, not research, claim permission, review, or publication.

## Validate the inbound package

Locate and read the complete authorised package before drafting. It must identify:

- the asset specification, audience, and intended outcome;
- the final permitted claim-and-evidence set;
- the research or decision synthesis;
- the source manifest and every material source's role;
- limitations and unresolved questions;
- destination communication and editorial authorities;
- authorised draft and production-record paths; and
- the acceptance owner.

Do not repair an incomplete package by browsing, launching research, classifying sources, or making a destination decision. Apply the four-part pre-drafting gate in `SKILL.md`. Before that gate passes, capture only clearly labelled working fragments within the authorised topic and write boundary; those fragments do not become permitted candidate material merely because they were captured.

## Explore the material

Explore before choosing an article order. Mine the package and authorised operator contributions for useful raw material such as:

- claims and supporting evidence;
- examples, vignettes, observations, and tensions;
- questions, analogies, sharp sentences, and useful distinctions;
- terminology conditions and qualifications; and
- candidate leading phrases that name an authorised idea more precisely.

Keep each factual or canonical fragment connected to its source role and claim permission. Treat operator speech and edits as production input, not automatic business or research authority. Label any newly surfaced factual proposition unresolved and keep it outside the permitted pile until its owner authorises it.

Do not impose an outline while exploring. The purpose is to widen and sharpen the authorised material before committing to a reader journey.

## Freeze the material pile

Store the pile in the destination's authorised production record. Give it an identifiable version and record:

- its authorised inputs and provenance;
- which material is permitted, unresolved, or excluded;
- any material limitation that composition must preserve; and
- the pile version from which each candidate path begins.

Once composition starts, treat the pile as fixed. If the emerging journey needs missing material, request an authorised contribution, cut the move, or explicitly return to exploration. Returning to exploration creates a new pile version before composition resumes.

Use the pile as a quarry, not a script. Split, combine, paraphrase, or omit fragments as the reader journey requires. Completion depends on fulfilling the production contract, not exhausting the pile.

## Choose Shape or Beats

Choose one exploitation branch and record why it fits:

- **Shape** is the default for institutional, analytical, explanatory, and argument-led work. Grow the reader journey as connected blocks and sections.
- **Beats** is for narrative, experiential, chronological, or journey-led work. Grow the piece one reader-facing move at a time.

They are alternatives, not sequential stages. Do not use Beats as an outline that Shape later expands. If the branch no longer fits, start a new candidate path from the same pile version or return explicitly to exploration.

## Ground the reader

Record a compact grounding ledger for material concepts only:

- **prerequisites** are concepts the named audience can reasonably be expected to bring;
- **introduced concepts** are concepts an earlier candidate unit has made usable.

A proposed unit is reachable only when every concept it requires is already a prerequisite or has been introduced. If a load-bearing concept is missing, ground it before the dependent move or remove that move. Do not over-explain familiar concepts merely to make the ledger exhaustive.

## Agree the entry path

For Consequential work, offer two or three genuinely different openings or entry paths. Vary the reader's entry point, emphasis, or form while preserving the authorised thesis and claim permissions. For each option, state:

- what it promises the reader;
- what later moves it enables; and
- any meaningful trade-off.

Let the operator choose or form an authorised hybrid. Do not let opening selection strengthen the thesis or broaden the permitted claim set.

For Routine work with a locked route, offer one proposed opening and invite correction. Generate alternatives only when a material editorial choice remains.

## Compose incrementally

Keep reader-facing copy separate from production notes. Before every write:

1. re-read the current candidate from disk;
2. identify operator edits, deletions, or reordering since the prior write;
3. preserve those changes as authoritative production input; and
4. re-check authority and candidate lineage if an edit changes a material claim or opening promise.

For **Shape**, once the production contract, reader journey, and entry path are agreed, draft one coherent section at a time. Within the section, do not ask for approval after every paragraph. Pause before:

- a load-bearing editorial choice;
- a sensitive or uncertain claim;
- an evidence or grounding gap; or
- an operator-requested checkpoint.

If one of those issues remains unresolved, keep the next write to one block. Before a material block, determine its primary job, what it adds beyond the prior block, and which form best performs that job: prose, list, table, callout, quotation, example, or another destination-permitted form.

For **Beats**, offer two or three reachable next moves. State what each move grounds or unlocks, let the operator choose, and write only the selected beat. Re-read the candidate and repeat until the journey fulfils its promise.

Do not batch the whole asset before its route is agreed. End when the reader journey satisfies the production contract; leave unused fragments unused.

## Apply the analytical writing standard

For each material block, resolve internally:

- what the block claims;
- what permitted support it uses;
- what that support means;
- what qualifies the interpretation; and
- what the reader should understand or conclude next.

These are judgment prompts, not required paragraph fields or a fixed rhetorical sequence. A paragraph need not perform every move, but a material section must do more than deposit information.

Use six quality lenses while composing:

1. **Thesis progression:** each material section advances a proposition rather than merely covering a topic.
2. **Analytical value:** important evidence is interpreted rather than deposited.
3. **Judgment discipline:** observation, interpretation, implication, and qualification remain distinguishable where the argument depends on them.
4. **Calibrated certainty:** wording expresses no more confidence than the permitted support.
5. **Reader sophistication:** explain what this audience needs without rehearsing what it is expected to know.
6. **Restrained prose:** write precisely, economically, and without promotional inflation. Draw commercial or domain implications only when the production contract requires them.

Treat these as defect signals, not mechanical word or punctuation bans:

- generic scene-setting;
- evidence without interpretation;
- inflated claims;
- redundant section summaries;
- unnecessary explanation; and
- recap-only conclusions.

Follow destination communication authority for house voice and any approved examples. Do not imitate an investment bank, advisory firm, or other institution unless that destination has explicitly made it an authorised reference.

## Complete Develop

Develop is complete when:

- one identifiable candidate exists in an authorised destination path;
- the reader journey fulfils the production contract;
- material claims preserve source permissions and limitations;
- every material block earns its place;
- production notes remain outside reader-facing copy; and
- the exact candidate is ready for the production QA required by `SKILL.md`.

If the pile, evidence, authority, or write boundary cannot support completion, return `SOURCE_OR_DECISION_REQUIRED` through the stop contract in `SKILL.md`. Do not disguise a material gap with connective prose.
