Lesson 1 · Axcíon AI resource development

Route before you build.

Your notes are evidence of a need, not proof of a solution. This lesson teaches you how to decide which lifecycle owns that need before anyone drafts a skill, command, process or plan.

10–15 minutes One tangible win: choose the right first route Repository state: 2026-07-30

The one idea to remember

Your first decision is about ownership, not format.

A rough note might say “we need a drafting skill.” That is already a proposed mechanism. The real need could be “we need to produce consistent first drafts.” The repository treats those as different questions.

First decide who owns the outcome. Only then decide whether a skill, command, instruction, normal prompt, manual habit or no build at all is the smallest useful mechanism.

Notes describe friction. Qualification decides the mechanism.

Recognition skill

Put the need into one of three lanes.

1

Operating capability

Something a project needs to be able to do: prepare buyer emails, run a publishing process, or make a repeatable decision.

/work-loop "<operating outcome>"

Owner: the project and its operating outcome.

2

New AI artifact

A proposed skill, command, agent, hook, prompt, script, reference file or persistent instruction—or a material expansion of one.

/develop-ai-resource <need or brief>

Owner: the artifact lifecycle.

3

Bounded correction

A known defect or settled change to something that already exists. The owner depends on the size and the resource type.

/work-loop "<settled correction>"

Owner: the existing object and its correction path.

Why this matters: an operating capability may need an AI artifact, but the artifact is only one component. `/work-loop` keeps the outcome and adoption decision; `/develop-ai-resource` qualifies and builds the artifact.

From messy notes to a decision

The common lifecycle has five questions.

1. Capture & draft the brief→ 2. Route→ 3. Shape→ 4. Build & prove→ 5. Land
1 · Capture & draft

Preserve the raw need, then draft the brief—not the final resource.

Keep the notes as input. The first draft should extract the desired outcome, evidence, examples, trigger conditions, exclusions and open assumptions. Do not turn the notes directly into a finished skill or command. If the input is a messy idea dump, use:

/leverage-idea path/to/notes.md

It distils the notes, checks evidence, generates distinct options, recommends one and creates a self-contained handoff. It does not implement.

What “drafting” means in this lifecycle: draft the problem statement, brief and proposed structure before the implementation plan. Writing the final SKILL.md or command body is already implementation.
2 · Route

Name the owner before the mechanism.

`/leverage-idea` classifies the domain. An operating outcome goes to `/work-loop`; a new or materially expanded AI artifact goes to `/develop-ai-resource`; a settled skill improvement goes to `/improve-skill`.

Reason: this prevents a plausible artifact from becoming the answer to a problem that belongs to a project, a habit, a normal prompt or an existing resource.
3 · Shape

Turn the need into a falsifiable plan.

A useful plan states the outcome, files or surfaces touched, build order, boundaries, evidence and what would prove the approach wrong. “Create a skill” is an action; “the skill triggers for X, does not trigger for Y, and produces Z” is a testable plan.

For an operating capability, this is the `/work-loop` Shape phase. For a rough AI-resource idea, `/leverage-idea` writes the first implementation plan and a raw brief; `/develop-ai-resource` then qualifies whether that plan should become a durable artifact.

4 · Build & prove

Implement the smallest approved slice, then observe it.

`/work-loop` builds one slice at a time and later proves the stream against Shape's falsification criteria. `/develop-ai-resource` builds one minimum candidate, asks whether it is well made, belongs in the system and does what it claims, then simplifies it.

A file existing is not runtime evidence. A command claiming it checks something is not evidence that the check ran. Record what was executed and what was observed.
5 · Land

Make a lifecycle decision, not a vague “done.”

A candidate can ship, revise, defer or be deleted. A capability can be adopted, held or rejected. No build, reuse and deferral are valid outcomes when the evidence supports them.

The operator owns adoption. The AI can recommend and demonstrate; it does not quietly turn a candidate into policy.

When something already exists

Choose the fix path by uncertainty and consequence.

What you have Use Why
A one-line cosmetic correction /tweak Small, explicit, rapid path; no structural redesign.
A settled change to an existing skill /improve-skill The skill, requested improvement and mechanism are already known.
A settled correction to an existing command, agent, script, hook or doc /work-loop It verifies premises, sizes the route, implements and returns evidence.
An unclear symptom with no trusted diagnosis /resolve-repo-problem Investigates and plans without applying a fix.
A mid-session fault to diagnose and fix now /resolve-incident Diagnosis, bounded fix, verification receipt and incident record.
An improvement that may change the mechanism or materially expand the resource /develop-ai-resource The need and system fit must be qualified again before building.
Stop condition: if you cannot state whether the problem is a known correction or a new capability, do not choose `/improve-skill` merely because a skill is nearby. Start with investigation or qualification.

Current review policy

Use one independent review layer, sized to consequence.

Small or mechanical

No model review. Verify deterministically by read-back, grep or execution.

Normal, consequential

One independent Codex review of the result after deterministic evidence exists.

High-consequence

One risk-aware Codex review before implementation, then retain execution safeguards.

Transition note, 2026-07-30: the review-layer consolidation was adopted on 2026-07-29, but some callers still describe older automatic or stacked review behavior. For review policy, follow docs/qc-independence.md. Inside `/work-loop`, follow docs/work-loop.md when its command text disagrees with the contract. Surface the mismatch rather than silently combining both procedures.

A build engine's own skill evaluation is part of that engine; it is not permission to stack several general reviews. The independent review exists to catch premise, scope and system-fit errors that the authoring process is least likely to see in its own work.

Temporary manual bridge outside `/work-loop`: if the approved plan creates a new command or skill, or otherwise falls into a high-consequence class, hand that plan to Codex once for the risk-aware review before allowing the build engine to implement. For a normal consequential change, give Codex the implemented result plus deterministic evidence once. Do not add `/qc-pass` or `/risk-check` on top unless the operator deliberately chooses one as the documented substitute or specialist check.

Retrieval practice

Choose the owner before revealing the answer.

Say your answer aloud or write it down. The effort of retrieving the route is what turns this from a readable guide into a usable skill.

Scenario A — “Here are four pages of ideas for improving our AI workflows.”

Start with /leverage-idea. The notes contain possible mechanisms but no settled owner. Its job is to distil, investigate, classify and hand off without implementing.

Scenario B — “This existing skill needs a clearer failure response; the change is fully specified.”

Use /improve-skill. The target, desired change and mechanism are settled, so repeating full artifact qualification would add no decision value.

Scenario C — “Our project must reliably prepare a buyer briefing before every call.”

Use /work-loop for an operating capability. A drafting skill may become one component later, but the project outcome and adoption decision remain with the capability.

Scenario D — “Add a new slash command that automatically writes shared state.”

Qualify the artifact through /develop-ai-resource. It is also high-consequence, so the current review policy requires a risk-aware independent review before implementation.

Scenario E — “The command has a typo in one sentence.”

Use /tweak. This is a bounded cosmetic correction; deterministic read-back is more proportionate than a general model review.

Your real-world exercise: take the next genuine note dump you produce. Before running any command, write one sentence naming the lane and why. Then compare your reasoning with the route `/leverage-idea` or `/work-loop` selects.
